# PTCB Exam Curriculum

## Goal

Organize PTCB exam preparation into short, engaging learning paths.

The app should break studying into small lessons that users can complete daily.

---

# Learning Categories

## 1. Medications

Topics:
- Brand names vs generic names
- Common medications
- Drug classifications
- Medication uses
- Common side effects
- Medication storage

---

## 2. Pharmacy Law and Regulations

Topics:
- Controlled substances
- DEA schedules
- HIPAA
- Prescription requirements
- Pharmacy regulations

---

## 3. Pharmacy Calculations

Topics:
- Dosage calculations
- Conversions
- Days supply
- Measurements
- Ratios and proportions

---

## 4. Patient Safety and Quality Assurance

Topics:
- Medication errors
- Safety procedures
- Infection control
- Quality improvement

---

## 5. Pharmacy Operations

Topics:
- Prescription processing
- Medication order entry
- Inventory management
- Billing and insurance basics

---

# Lesson Structure

Each lesson should contain:

1. Short explanation
2. Memory trick
3. Example
4. Practice questions
5. XP reward

---

# Difficulty Levels

Easy:
- Basic definitions
- Recognition questions

Medium:
- Application questions
- Multi-step problems

Hard:
- Exam-style scenarios
- Complex calculations
